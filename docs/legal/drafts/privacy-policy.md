# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026
Version: 2.0 (draft for legal review; not yet in force)
Supersedes: Privacy Policy dated August 2026 (website) and Privacy Policy dated April 2026 (booking app)

## At a glance

- We collect what we need to place a verified helper with your household: your name, phone, email, the city and locality you choose, your requirements, and payment references. We never see your card number, and we do not track your device's location.
- We share your name, contact number and requirements with a Helper only when you decide to interview or engage them. We share payment data with PayU, and we use Supabase, Vercel, Resend, Google, Chatwoot and OpenRouter to run the service. We do not sell personal data and we run no advertising.
- Our support chat is answered first by an automated assistant and then, when you need one, by a person. We keep a record of every support conversation, on chat, WhatsApp, phone and email, and we analyse those records in aggregate to improve the service. We do not build advertising profiles from them.
- Helpers are people too. We collect their identity, reference, assessment and police-verification data, share a dossier with you before placement, and expect you to protect it.
- You can see, correct and delete your data. Delete My Account in the app erases your profile, bookings, plans and support conversations at once, except for records the law requires us to keep — chiefly financial and tax records, which are moved to a restricted archive.
- Complaints go to our Grievance Officer first, then to the Data Protection Board of India. If you are in the EU, UK or California, and those laws reach the processing, the extra rights in Section 14 apply to you.

## 7. How we protect personal data {#security}

> **Summary:** Encryption in transit and at rest, database rules that let each user read only their own data, restricted administrative access, and provider certifications. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board.

7.1 **Measures.** We apply security safeguards appropriate to the data we hold, as Section 8(5) of the DPDP Act requires, including:

- (b) row-level access rules in our database, so that a signed-in user can read and write only their own profile, requests and plan records;
