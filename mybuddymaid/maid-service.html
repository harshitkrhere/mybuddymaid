What Does a Professional Maid Do?

A professional maid from MyBuddyMaid handles every aspect of daily housekeeping so that your home stays spotless without you lifting a finger. Our maids are trained in systematic cleaning routines, proper use of cleaning agents on different surfaces, and efficient time management to ensure every task gets done within the agreed hours. Here is a detailed breakdown of what our maids take care of:

Daily Sweeping & Mopping

Thorough sweeping and wet-mopping of all rooms, hallways, and balconies using appropriate cleaners for marble, vitrified tiles, wooden flooring, and mosaic surfaces. Includes moving lightweight furniture to clean underneath.

Dusting & Surface Wiping

Dusting all furniture, shelves, window sills, photo frames, showpieces, TV units, and electronic appliances using microfibre cloths to avoid scratches. Ceiling fans and light fixtures are dusted on a weekly rotation.

Utensil Washing & Kitchen Cleanup

Washing all cooking vessels, plates, glasses, and cutlery after meals. Cleaning the kitchen platform, gas stove, chimney exterior, and sink area. Wiping down the fridge exterior and microwave.

Laundry — Washing, Drying & Folding

Hand-washing delicate garments, loading and unloading the washing machine, hanging clothes to dry on the balcony or terrace, and neatly folding and organizing dried clothes in wardrobes.

Bathroom & Toilet Cleaning

Scrubbing toilet bowls, wash basins, shower areas, and bathroom tiles with disinfectant. Cleaning mirrors, replacing toiletries, and ensuring bathroom floors are dry to prevent slipping.

Bed Making & Linen Change

Making beds every morning, fluffing pillows, and changing bed sheets, pillow covers, and mattress protectors on a weekly schedule or as needed.

Ironing & Wardrobe Organization

Pressing clothes using a steam or dry iron, hanging formal wear on hangers, folding casual clothing, and organizing wardrobes seasonally — packing away winter or summer clothes as appropriate.

Trash Segregation & Disposal

Collecting garbage from all rooms, segregating wet and dry waste as per municipal guidelines, and handing over trash to the building's collection system or municipal pickup.

Grocery Receiving & Pantry Stocking

Receiving grocery deliveries from BigBasket, Zepto, or Blinkit, checking items against the order list, and stocking them properly in the pantry, fridge, or designated storage areas.

Pooja Room & Special Area Maintenance

Cleaning the pooja room or mandir area, wiping idols gently, replacing old flowers, and maintaining the cleanliness of prayer items — handled with appropriate cultural sensitivity.

Guest-Ready Deep Cleaning

Preparing the home before guests arrive — extra mopping, arranging guest bedrooms, cleaning the guest bathroom, laying out fresh towels, and a general tidying of common areas.

Pet Area Cleaning

For homes with pets — cleaning pet feeding bowls, sweeping pet hair from sofas and floors, wiping paw prints from entryways, and maintaining the pet sleeping area.

Types of Maid Service

Part-Time Maid (2-4 Hours/Day)

Our most popular option for nuclear families and working couples. A part-time maid visits your home daily for 2 to 4 hours, handling core tasks like sweeping, mopping, utensil washing, and laundry. She works on a fixed morning or evening slot that suits your schedule. Perfect for apartments and smaller homes where daily upkeep is all that is needed.

Ideal for: Working couples, bachelor flatshares, 1-2 BHK apartments, families with minimal cooking at home

Full-Time Maid (8-12 Hours/Day)

A full-time maid stays at your home for the entire day but returns to her own residence at night. She handles all cleaning, cooking assistance, laundry, ironing, and any ad-hoc household tasks. This option is ideal for larger families or homes where there is always something that needs attention — from receiving deliveries to managing vendors.

Ideal for: Joint families, 3+ BHK homes, families with young children, homes with frequent guests

Live-In Maid (24/7 Availability)

A live-in maid resides at your home and is available throughout the day and for light duties in the evening. She gets her own room or designated sleeping area, meals, and weekly off. This arrangement works best for large households, NRI families visiting India periodically, and families who need round-the-clock domestic support without managing multiple part-time helpers.

Ideal for: NRI families, bungalows/villas, large joint families, households needing 24/7 availability, families with elderly members

Red Flags When Hiring a Maid

  • 🚩 Reluctance to share identity documents such as Aadhaar card or voter ID — genuine candidates have nothing to hide
  • 🚩 Frequent job changes with gaps that cannot be explained — may indicate reliability issues or conflicts with previous employers
  • 🚩 Refusing a trial period or insisting on advance salary before starting work
  • 🚩 No verifiable references from previous employers — always call at least one reference
  • 🚩 Agencies that charge the maid a hefty commission, which often leads to the maid quitting within weeks to avoid repayment
  • 🚩 Unwillingness to agree to fixed working hours or specific task lists — leads to misunderstandings later
  • 🚩 Agencies that refuse to provide a written agreement or placement receipt
  • 🚩 Candidates who are evasive about their permanent home address or provide inconsistent personal details

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a maid cost in India in 2026?

Full-time and live-in maids cost significantly more — ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 per month in metros.

For part-time maids working 2-4 hours, providing food is optional but appreciated — a cup of tea and a snack is a common courtesy in most Indian households. For full-time maids working 8+ hours, providing lunch is standard practice. For live-in maids, all meals must be provided as part of the arrangement.

What tasks can I expect a part-time maid to do?
A part-time maid from MyBuddyMaid handles sweeping, mopping, dusting, utensil washing, bathroom cleaning, laundry (washing, drying, folding), bed making, and basic kitchen cleanup. The exact task list is agreed upon before placement and can be adjusted during the trial period. Tasks like cooking, ironing, and deep cleaning are typically add-ons or require extended hours.

In most metro and tier-2 cities, we can deploy a verified maid within 24 hours of receiving your request. What is the difference between a maid and a domestic helper?
In practical terms, there is no difference — both refer to someone who helps with household chores. The term 'domestic helper' is increasingly preferred as it is more respectful. At MyBuddyMaid, we use both terms interchangeably. Regardless of the title, our helpers are trained professionals who take pride in their work and deserve to be treated with dignity.
Do I need to give a maid weekly off and festivals off?
Yes. As per fair employment practices and the guidelines recommended by domestic worker welfare organizations, part-time maids should receive at least 4 days off per month (typically Sundays). Full-time and live-in maids must get one weekly off and all major national and regional festival holidays. Diwali, Eid, Pongal, Durga Puja, and Christmas are standard offs. A festival bonus equal to one month's salary is customary.
