Tips for Jaipur Families

  • Start early — Good domestic help gets snapped up quickly in Jaipur. Begin your search at least 2 weeks before you need someone.
  • Verify always — Jaipur's large, transient population makes background verification absolutely critical.
  • Consider commute — A maid living nearby is more likely to be punctual and reliable long-term.
  • Use UPI payments — Transparent digital payments build trust and create records.
  • Budget for festivals — Diwali/Chhath/Eid bonuses are standard and expected in Jaipur.
