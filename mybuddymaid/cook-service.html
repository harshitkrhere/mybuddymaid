What Does a Professional Home Cook Do?

A cook from MyBuddyMaid does much more than just prepare food. They manage your entire meal cycle — from planning menus and managing ingredients to cooking, plating, and cleaning up the kitchen. Our cooks are trained in food safety, proper storage techniques, and efficient kitchen management to minimize waste and maximize nutrition.

Daily Meal Preparation (Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner)

Preparing complete meals including rice, dal, roti/paratha/naan, 2-3 sabzis, salad, and accompaniments like raita, chutney, or pickle. Menu is planned weekly in consultation with the family to ensure variety.

Cuisine-Specific Cooking

Specializing in the cuisine of your choice — Punjabi, Rajasthani, South Indian, Bengali, Gujarati, Maharashtrian, or Continental. Many of our cooks are proficient in multiple regional cuisines and can rotate styles throughout the week.

Tiffin & Lunchbox Preparation

Preparing packed lunches for working adults and school-going children. Ensuring tiffin is ready and packed before the family leaves in the morning, with attention to items that stay fresh for hours — parathas, rice dishes, sandwiches, and fruits.

Diet & Health-Specific Meal Prep

Cooking as per dietary restrictions — diabetic-friendly low-sugar meals, low-oil heart-healthy food, high-protein gym diets, pregnancy nutrition plans, Jain cooking, sattvic food, or gluten-free preparations. Menus are designed with inputs from your nutritionist or doctor.

Grocery & Ingredient Management

Monitoring pantry stock levels, creating weekly grocery lists, checking freshness of vegetables and dairy, and coordinating orders from local vendors or delivery apps. Ensuring spice boxes (masala dabba) and staples are always stocked.

Kitchen Hygiene & Cleanup

Cleaning the kitchen platform, gas stove, chimney, and sink after every cooking session. Sanitizing cutting boards, washing all utensils used during cooking, and mopping the kitchen floor. Ensuring dustbin is emptied and waste is segregated.

Snack & Chai Preparation

Preparing morning and evening tea or coffee as per family preference — masala chai, filter coffee, green tea, or herbal infusions. Making snacks like poha, upma, sandwiches, sprout chaat, or pakodas for teatime.

Festival & Occasion Cooking

Preparing special meals for festivals — Diwali sweets like gulab jamun and ladoo, Eid biryani and sewaiyaan, Pongal and payasam, Navratri fasting food (kuttu ki puri, sabudana khichdi), and Christmas cake. Also managing cooking for small family gatherings of 15-25 guests.

Preservation & Storage

Properly storing cooked food in airtight containers, managing fridge organization to prevent cross-contamination, making weekly batches of chutneys, pickles, masala pastes, and frozen chapati dough to save time during busy weeks.

Baby Food Preparation

For families with toddlers — preparing age-appropriate, nutrient-rich meals like mashed dal-rice, ragi porridge, vegetable purees, soft khichdi, and fresh fruit mixes. All baby food is prepared with extra attention to hygiene and allergen avoidance.

Types of Cook Service

Part-Time Cook (1-2 Meals/Day)

A cook visits your home for 2-3 hours daily to prepare one or two meals — typically lunch and dinner, or breakfast and lunch. This is our most affordable option and works great for working couples who eat out for one meal but want home-cooked food for the rest.

Ideal for: Working couples, bachelors/bachelorettes, small families with 2-4 members

Full-Time Cook (All Meals + Snacks)

A cook who works 6-8 hours in your kitchen, handling all three meals, morning/evening tea, snacks, tiffin packing, grocery management, and kitchen cleanup. This is ideal for larger families where the volume of cooking is substantial and variety is important.

Ideal for: Joint families, families with 5+ members, homes with diverse dietary needs (elders need different food from children)

Live-In Cook (Residential)

A cook who lives on your premises and is available for all meals, guest entertaining, and special requests. Live-in cooks often double as kitchen managers, handling grocery shopping, pantry organization, and vendor relationships. They are typically experienced professionals with 10+ years in the field.

Ideal for: HNI families, farmhouses, large bungalows, families that entertain frequently, NRI homes during extended visits

Red Flags When Hiring a Home Cook

  • 🚩 Cook arrives for the trial without washing hands or wearing a clean apron — hygiene habits are ingrained, not taught on the job
  • 🚩 Claims to cook 'everything' but cannot name specific dishes from any regional cuisine — jack of all trades, master of none
  • 🚩 Uses excessive oil, salt, or MSG-based flavour enhancers to make food taste better quickly
  • 🚩 Refuses to cook non-vegetarian food if your family eats it, or vice versa — dietary compatibility must be established upfront
  • 🚩 History of pilfering groceries or inflating grocery bills at previous employment — ask references specifically about this
  • 🚩 Cannot prepare basic staples like round rotis, properly cooked rice, or a balanced dal — the fundamentals must be strong
  • 🚩 Insists on using only their own recipes and refuses to adapt to your family's taste preferences
  • 🚩 Leaves the kitchen dirty after cooking and considers cleanup someone else's job

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a home cook cost in India?
Part-time cook salaries range from ₹8,000 to ₹18,000 per month for 1-2 meals daily. Full-time cooks handling all meals cost ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 depending on the city and cuisine expertise. Specialized diet cooks (diabetic, keto, Jain) and those proficient in Continental or Chinese cooking charge a 20-30% premium. In metro cities like Mumbai and Delhi, expect to be at the higher end of these ranges.
Can I hire a cook who makes only vegetarian food?
Absolutely. We have a large pool of cooks who specialize exclusively in vegetarian and Jain cooking. Many come from backgrounds where they have only cooked vegetarian food their entire career. We can also match you with cooks who specialize in sattvic cooking for families that follow strict dietary codes based on Ayurvedic principles.
What cuisines do your cooks specialize in?
Our cook network includes specialists in North Indian (Punjabi, Rajasthani, UP, Kashmiri), South Indian (Tamil, Kerala, Andhra, Karnataka), East Indian (Bengali, Odia, Assamese), West Indian (Gujarati, Maharashtrian, Goan), as well as Continental, Chinese, and fusion cuisines. We also have cooks trained in specific dietary cooking — keto, high-protein, diabetic-friendly, and pregnancy nutrition.
Will the cook also clean the kitchen after cooking?
Yes. Our standard agreement includes complete kitchen cleanup after every cooking session — washing all cooking utensils, cleaning the gas stove and platform, mopping the kitchen floor, and organizing leftover ingredients. The kitchen should be cleaner after the cook leaves than before they started.
Can the cook prepare meals for guests or small parties?
Our full-time and live-in cooks can comfortably handle meals for gatherings of up to 15-25 guests. For larger events, we recommend hiring additional temporary kitchen help through our network. If you frequently entertain, let us know during the matching process — we will prioritize cooks with catering experience.
How do I ensure the cook is not wasting or stealing groceries?
We establish a simple grocery management system — the cook maintains a daily register of items used, and a weekly grocery budget is agreed upon. You can verify quantities against actual dishes prepared. Our monthly check-ins specifically cover grocery management. Over time, most families develop a trusted routine where these checks become routine but effective.
Can the cook prepare baby food and toddler meals?
Yes. We have cooks experienced in preparing age-appropriate meals for infants and toddlers — from ragi porridge and mashed vegetables for 6-month-olds to finger foods, mini parathas, and mild curries for toddlers. If your paediatrician has provided a diet chart, the cook will follow it precisely.
What if I don't like the cook's food after a few weeks?
First, provide specific feedback — is it too oily, too spicy, too bland? Our team will convey this to the cook and give them a chance to adjust. If the food quality still does not match your expectations after a reasonable adjustment period, our replacement guarantee allows you to get a new cook at no additional charge.
Does the cook buy groceries or do I have to provide them?
This is flexible. Some families prefer to order groceries themselves through BigBasket or Blinkit and have the cook work with what is available. Others give the cook a weekly budget and let them shop from local vendors for the freshest vegetables, meat, and dairy. We recommend starting with family-provided groceries and transitioning to cook-managed shopping once trust is established.
Can I hire a cook just for weekends?
Yes, we offer weekend-only cook placements. This is popular with working professionals who manage quick weekday meals themselves but want elaborate, home-cooked meals on Saturday and Sunday — think full thalis, special dishes, and meals for the extended family. Weekend cook rates are typically ₹4,000-₹8,000 per month for Saturday-Sunday service.
