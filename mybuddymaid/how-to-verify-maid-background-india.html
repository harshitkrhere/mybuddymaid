How to Verify a Maid's Background in India — Complete Checklist

Step-by-step guide to conducting thorough background checks on domestic help before hiring them in 2026.

Hiring someone to work in your home — where your family, children, and valuables are — without proper background verification is a risk no Indian family should take. Yet millions do it every day, relying on a watchman's recommendation or a neighbor's referral. This guide walks you through the complete verification process you should follow before allowing anyone into your home.

The 7-Step Verification Checklist

  1. Aadhaar Card Verification

    Ask for the original Aadhaar card (not a photocopy). Verify the photo matches the person. Cross-check the Aadhaar number on the UIDAI website (mAadhaar app or resident.uidai.gov.in) to confirm it's genuine. Note down the Aadhaar number for your records.

  2. Additional ID Documents

    Request at least one more ID: Voter ID, PAN card, or ration card. Multiple IDs help confirm the person's identity and prevent impersonation.

  3. Police Verification

    Visit your local police station and request a tenant/domestic help verification. You'll need the maid's Aadhaar copy, photograph, and your own ID. The police will verify their records and issue a clearance certificate. This takes 1-3 weeks through normal channels.

  4. Reference Check — Call Previous Employers

    Ask for phone numbers of at least 2 previous employers. Call them and ask: How long did she work? Why did she leave? Was anything ever missing? Was she punctual? Would you hire her again? Be wary if she can't provide any references.

  5. Address Verification

    Note her permanent and current address. For live-in placements, verify the permanent address through a field visit or trusted local contact. Know where your domestic help lives — it's essential for accountability.

  6. Photograph & Emergency Contact

    Take a clear photograph on the first day. Ask for an emergency contact number (family member). Share these with your building security/RWA for their records.

  7. Written Agreement

    Create a simple written agreement covering: duties, working hours, salary, weekly off, notice period, and termination conditions. Both parties sign. This protects you and the maid.

Red Flags During Verification

  • 🚩 Reluctance to share Aadhaar or any ID documents
  • 🚩 Aadhaar photo doesn't match the person
  • 🚩 Cannot provide any previous employer references
  • 🚩 Inconsistent information about address or work history
  • 🚩 Demands advance salary before starting work
  • 🚩 Refuses a trial period
  • 🚩 Previous employers give vague or evasive answers
