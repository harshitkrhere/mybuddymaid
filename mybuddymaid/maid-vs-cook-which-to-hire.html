Maid vs Cook — Which Should You Hire First?

Both are essential for Indian households, but if your budget allows only one, here's how to decide.

It's the classic Indian household dilemma: you've decided to hire domestic help, but your budget allows only one person to start with. Should you hire a maid (for cleaning, laundry, and housekeeping) or a cook (for fresh home-cooked meals)? The answer depends on your lifestyle, family size, and what drains your energy the most.

Hire a Maid First If...

  • You enjoy cooking or one partner handles meals
  • You live in a larger home (2+ BHK) that needs daily maintenance
  • You have allergies or respiratory issues that require a dust-free environment
  • You order food delivery only 2-3 times a week — not daily
  • Your biggest time drain is cleaning, laundry, and housekeeping

Hire a Cook First If...

  • Neither partner has time or interest in cooking
  • You have young children or elderly family members who need home-cooked meals
  • You're spending ₹10,000+ monthly on Swiggy/Zomato food delivery
  • You or family members have specific dietary needs (diabetic, Jain, low-oil)
  • Your biggest daily stress is figuring out what to eat

Our Verdict

For working couples without children in a 1-2 BHK apartment: hire a maid first. Cleaning is daily and unavoidable, while you can manage with simple cooking or occasional delivery.

For families with children or elderly parents: hire a cook first. Nutritious home meals directly impact health, and children need consistent, balanced food. You can manage cleaning on weekends or with a 2x/week deep clean.

FAQs

Can one person do both cooking and cleaning?
It's possible but not ideal. A maid who also cooks typically does neither task to a high standard. Cooking requires focus and culinary skills, while cleaning requires stamina and attention to detail. If budget is tight, hire a cook and manage basic cleaning yourself on weekdays with a weekly deep-clean maid.
How much does a maid + cook combo cost in India?
In metro cities, a part-time maid (2-3 hrs) + part-time cook (2 hrs) costs ₹16,000-₹28,000/month combined. MyBuddyMaid offers combo packages starting at ₹12,000/month with verified professionals. In tier-2 cities, the combo ranges from ₹10,000-₹20,000.
Should the maid and cook be different people?
Yes, we strongly recommend separate people for cooking and cleaning. This ensures specialization — your cook focuses entirely on meal quality and hygiene, while your maid focuses on housekeeping. It also provides backup: if one is absent, the other still shows up.
