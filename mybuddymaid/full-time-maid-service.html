What Does a Full-Time Live-In Maid Do?

A live-in maid from MyBuddyMaid is trained to handle the complete spectrum of household management. Unlike a part-time helper who focuses on specific tasks, a live-in maid integrates into your household rhythm and manages everything from morning routines to evening wrap-up. She is the backbone of a well-run home.

Complete Daily Housekeeping

All sweeping, mopping, dusting, and surface cleaning across every room in the house — including rarely used guest rooms, storage areas, and terraces. A thorough deep clean of one room is done on rotation each day.

Meal Preparation & Kitchen Management

Preparing breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and dinner for the entire family. Managing the pantry, tracking supplies that are running low, and coordinating grocery orders. Cleaning the kitchen thoroughly after every meal.

Full Laundry Cycle Management

Sorting clothes by fabric and colour, loading the washing machine, hand-washing delicates, drying, ironing, and organizing pressed clothes back into wardrobes. Managing seasonal clothing storage and dry cleaning pickups.

Childcare Assistance

Supervising young children during the day, preparing their meals and snacks, helping with school uniform preparation, accompanying children to the school bus stop, and engaging them in age-appropriate activities when parents are busy.

Elderly Family Member Support

Assisting elderly family members with daily needs — serving meals on time, reminding them about medications, helping with mobility within the house, and providing companionship during the day when other family members are at work.

Vendor & Delivery Management

Receiving all deliveries — groceries, courier packages, water cans, gas cylinders. Coordinating with the dhobi, plumber, electrician, and other service providers. Managing interactions with the building maintenance staff.

Guest Preparation & Hospitality

Preparing guest bedrooms with fresh linen, cleaning guest bathrooms, arranging refreshments and snacks for visitors, serving tea and meals to guests, and ensuring the home is in impeccable condition when company is expected.

Festival & Event Preparation

Deep cleaning the home before festivals like Diwali, Navratri, Eid, or Pongal. Helping with rangoli preparation, decorating the pooja room, and supporting the family during events like housewarming ceremonies, birthday parties, or puja gatherings.

Garden & Outdoor Maintenance

Watering plants, sweeping the porch, driveway, and garden pathways. Maintaining balcony gardens, wiping outdoor furniture, and ensuring the car parking area stays clean.

Pet Care

Feeding household pets on schedule, cleaning food and water bowls, sweeping pet hair, taking dogs for short walks within the society compound, and maintaining pet bedding and play areas.

Nighttime Duties & Security Awareness

Locking all doors and windows at night, switching off unnecessary lights and fans, ensuring the gas stove is turned off, and checking that water purifiers and filters are refilled for the morning.

Property Caretaking (For NRI Families)

For absentee homeowners: maintaining the home in lived-in condition, running taps and faucets regularly to prevent pipe corrosion, airing out rooms, managing society maintenance payments, and coordinating with property managers.

Types of Full-Time Live-In Maid Service

Full-Time Day Maid (8-12 Hours)

A maid who arrives early morning and stays until evening but goes to her own home at night. She handles all daytime household tasks including cooking, cleaning, laundry, and childcare assistance. This option works well for families who want extensive help during the day but prefer privacy at night.

Ideal for: Working couples with children, medium-sized families, homes without a separate servant quarter

Live-In Maid (24/7 Residential)

A maid who lives on your premises full-time, available throughout the day and for essential tasks in the evening. She gets a designated room, all meals, and one weekly off. This is the most comprehensive household support option and works best when you need someone always available.

Ideal for: Large joint families, bungalows/villas, NRI property management, families with elderly members needing night-time assistance

Couple Placement (Husband-Wife Team)

A married couple is placed together — typically the wife handles indoor duties (cooking, cleaning, laundry) while the husband manages outdoor tasks (gardening, driving, security, vendor management). They share a room on your premises. This is the gold standard for large, well-staffed households.

Ideal for: Farmhouses, luxury villas, large bungalows, families needing both indoor and outdoor help

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a live-in maid cost in India in 2026?
Live-in maid salaries in India range from ₹15,000 to ₹35,000 per month depending on the city, experience, and scope of duties. In Mumbai and Delhi NCR, experienced live-in maids who can also cook charge ₹22,000-₹35,000. In cities like Pune, Ahmedabad, and Hyderabad, the range is ₹15,000-₹25,000. This is the cash salary — accommodation and meals are provided separately by the employer.
What facilities should I provide to a live-in maid?
At minimum, you should provide a separate, clean room with a bed, fan (or AC in extremely hot cities), and a lockable cupboard for personal belongings. Three meals a day, access to a bathroom, and basic toiletries should also be provided. Many families also provide a basic mobile phone for communication, a TV in the maid's room, and new clothes during festivals as a goodwill gesture.
How many days off should a live-in maid get?
A live-in maid should receive at least 4 days off per month — typically every Sunday. Additionally, all major national holidays (Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti) and regional festivals (Diwali, Eid, Pongal, Durga Puja, Onam, Christmas) should be given as paid holidays. Many families allow 7-10 days of annual leave for the maid to visit her hometown, usually around major festivals.
Can a live-in maid cook as well?
Many of our live-in maids can handle basic Indian cooking — dal, rice, sabzi, roti, and South Indian staples like sambar and rasam. However, if you need an expert cook who can prepare elaborate meals, we recommend pairing a live-in maid with our dedicated Cook Service. Alternatively, we can match you with a live-in maid who has specific culinary skills — though these candidates command higher salaries.
Is it safe to have a live-in maid when I have young children?
With proper verification, absolutely. All our live-in maids undergo enhanced background checks specifically designed for households with children. We screen for any criminal history, conduct behavioural assessments, and check references specifically asking about interactions with kids. That said, a live-in maid is household help, not a dedicated nanny. If you need focused childcare, we recommend adding our Nanny Service.
What if my live-in maid wants to leave suddenly?
Our placement agreement requires a 15-day notice period from both parties. If a maid leaves without notice, our 1-year replacement guarantee kicks in — we deploy a replacement within 48-72 hours at no extra charge. We also maintain an emergency backup pool of pre-verified candidates for exactly these situations.
I am an NRI — can I hire a live-in maid to manage my empty house in India?
Yes, this is one of our most popular use cases. We place live-in caretakers for NRI-owned properties across India. The maid maintains the home, keeps it clean and aired out, manages society maintenance payments, coordinates with property managers, and ensures the house is move-in ready whenever you visit. We provide monthly photo/video reports and your relationship manager conducts regular inspections.
How do I handle salary for a live-in maid — cash or bank transfer?
We strongly recommend monthly bank transfers. It creates a transparent financial trail for both parties, helps the maid build a banking history, and simplifies any future disputes about pending payments. Most of our maids have bank accounts — if they do not, we help them open one during onboarding. You can also use UPI for salary transfers.
Can I set CCTV cameras in my home with a live-in maid?
You can install CCTV cameras in common areas like the living room, kitchen, and children's playroom. However, cameras should never be placed in the maid's room, bathroom, or changing areas — this is a violation of privacy and potentially illegal. We recommend informing the maid about camera placement during onboarding. Transparency builds trust and helps both parties feel secure.
What is the ideal room setup for a live-in maid?
The room should have a proper bed (not a floor mattress), a fan or cooler, a lockable wardrobe or cupboard, adequate lighting, and ideally a small window for ventilation. Access to a separate or shared bathroom is essential. Many families set up a small shelf or table for personal items. A happy maid with dignified living conditions is far more likely to stay long-term and perform well.
