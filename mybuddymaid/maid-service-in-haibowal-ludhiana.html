How much does a maid cost in India in 2026?
Part-time maid salaries in India range from ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 per month depending on the city and working hours. In metro cities like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru, expect to pay ₹8,000-₹15,000 for 2-4 hours daily. In tier-2 cities like Jaipur, Lucknow, and Chandigarh, the range is ₹5,000-₹10,000. Full-time and live-in maids cost significantly more — ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 per month in metros.

Do I need to provide food to a part-time maid?
For part-time maids working 2-4 hours, providing food is optional but appreciated — a cup of tea and a snack is a common courtesy in most Indian households. For full-time maids working 8+ hours, providing lunch is standard practice. For live-in maids, all meals must be provided as part of the arrangement.

What tasks can I expect a part-time maid to do?
A part-time maid from MyBuddyMaid handles sweeping, mopping, dusting, utensil washing, bathroom cleaning, laundry (washing, drying, folding), bed making, and basic kitchen cleanup. The exact task list is agreed upon before placement and can be adjusted during the trial period. Tasks like cooking, ironing, and deep cleaning are typically add-ons or require extended hours.

What is the difference between a maid and a domestic helper?
In practical terms, there is no difference — both refer to someone who helps with household chores. The term 'domestic helper' is increasingly preferred as it is more respectful. At MyBuddyMaid, we use both terms interchangeably. Regardless of the title, our helpers are trained professionals who take pride in their work and deserve to be treated with dignity.
Do I need to give a maid weekly off and festivals off?
Yes. As per fair employment practices and the guidelines recommended by domestic worker welfare organizations, part-time maids should receive at least 4 days off per month (typically Sundays). Full-time and live-in maids must get one weekly off and all major national and regional festival holidays. Diwali, Eid, Pongal, Durga Puja, and Christmas are standard offs. A festival bonus equal to one month's salary is customary.
