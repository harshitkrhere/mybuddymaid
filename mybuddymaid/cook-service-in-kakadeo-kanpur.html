FAQs — Cook Service in Kakadeo

How much does a home cook cost in India?
Part-time cook salaries range from ₹8,000 to ₹18,000 per month for 1-2 meals daily. Full-time cooks handling all meals cost ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 depending on the city and cuisine expertise. Specialized diet cooks (diabetic, keto, Jain) and those proficient in Continental or Chinese cooking charge a 20-30% premium. In metro cities like Mumbai and Delhi, expect to be at the higher end of these ranges.
Can I hire a cook who makes only vegetarian food?
Absolutely. We have a large pool of cooks who specialize exclusively in vegetarian and Jain cooking. Many come from backgrounds where they have only cooked vegetarian food their entire career. We can also match you with cooks who specialize in sattvic cooking for families that follow strict dietary codes based on Ayurvedic principles.
What cuisines do your cooks specialize in?
Our cook network includes specialists in North Indian (Punjabi, Rajasthani, UP, Kashmiri), South Indian (Tamil, Kerala, Andhra, Karnataka), East Indian (Bengali, Odia, Assamese), West Indian (Gujarati, Maharashtrian, Goan), as well as Continental, Chinese, and fusion cuisines. We also have cooks trained in specific dietary cooking — keto, high-protein, diabetic-friendly, and pregnancy nutrition.
Will the cook also clean the kitchen after cooking?
Yes. Our standard agreement includes complete kitchen cleanup after every cooking session — washing all cooking utensils, cleaning the gas stove and platform, mopping the kitchen floor, and organizing leftover ingredients. The kitchen should be cleaner after the cook leaves than before they started.
Can the cook prepare meals for guests or small parties?
Our full-time and live-in cooks can comfortably handle meals for gatherings of up to 15-25 guests. For larger events, we recommend hiring additional temporary kitchen help through our network. If you frequently entertain, let us know during the matching process — we will prioritize cooks with catering experience.
How do I ensure the cook is not wasting or stealing groceries?
We establish a simple grocery management system — the cook maintains a daily register of items used, and a weekly grocery budget is agreed upon. You can verify quantities against actual dishes prepared. Our monthly check-ins specifically cover grocery management. Over time, most families develop a trusted routine where these checks become routine but effective.
Can the cook prepare baby food and toddler meals?
Yes. We have cooks experienced in preparing age-appropriate meals for infants and toddlers — from ragi porridge and mashed vegetables for 6-month-olds to finger foods, mini parathas, and mild curries for toddlers. If your paediatrician has provided a diet chart, the cook will follow it precisely.
What if I don't like the cook's food after a few weeks?
First, provide specific feedback — is it too oily, too spicy, too bland? Our team will convey this to the cook and give them a chance to adjust. If the food quality still does not match your expectations after a reasonable adjustment period, our replacement guarantee allows you to get a new cook at no additional charge.
Does the cook buy groceries or do I have to provide them?
This is flexible. Some families prefer to order groceries themselves through BigBasket or Blinkit and have the cook work with what is available. Others give the cook a weekly budget and let them shop from local vendors for the freshest vegetables, meat, and dairy. We recommend starting with family-provided groceries and transitioning to cook-managed shopping once trust is established.
Can I hire a cook just for weekends?
Yes, we offer weekend-only cook placements. This is popular with working professionals who manage quick weekday meals themselves but want elaborate, home-cooked meals on Saturday and Sunday — think full thalis, special dishes, and meals for the extended family. Weekend cook rates are typically ₹4,000-₹8,000 per month for Saturday-Sunday service.
