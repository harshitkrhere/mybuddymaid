Part-Time vs Full-Time Maid — Which is Right for You?

A detailed comparison of part-time (2-4 hrs) vs full-time (8-12 hrs) vs live-in maid arrangements for Indian households.

One of the first decisions when hiring domestic help in India is choosing between a part-time maid (2-4 hours daily), a full-time maid (8-12 hours daily), or a live-in maid (24/7 residential). Each arrangement has different cost structures, expectations, and advantages. Here's a complete guide to help you decide.

Part-Time Maid — Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • Most affordable option
  • Maximum privacy — she leaves after work
  • Easy to find and replace
  • No obligation for meals or accommodation
  • Multiple part-time maids = backup coverage

❌ Cons

  • Limited to basic tasks in 2-4 hours
  • No help for unexpected tasks outside hours
  • May work at multiple homes (punctuality issues)
  • You handle everything else yourself

Full-Time Maid — Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • Handles virtually all household tasks
  • Available for errands, deliveries, vendors
  • Can assist with childcare and elderly support
  • One person to manage instead of multiple part-timers

❌ Cons

  • More expensive than part-time
  • Present in your home all day (less privacy)
  • Need to provide lunch
  • Higher dependency — harder if she's absent

Live-In Maid — Pros & Cons

✅ Pros

  • Round-the-clock availability
  • Complete household management
  • Ideal for large homes and joint families
  • Security — someone always at home
  • Best for NRI property management

❌ Cons

  • Highest cost (salary + meals + room)
  • Minimal privacy
  • Must provide separate room and bathroom ideally
  • More complex relationship management
  • Weekly off management needed

Our Recommendation

Working couples in 1-2 BHK: Start with a part-time maid. It's affordable and covers daily essentials.

Families with children in 3+ BHK: Full-time maid. The all-day support is invaluable when managing kids, cooking, and household.

Large homes, joint families, NRI properties: Live-in maid (or couple placement). The investment pays for itself in peace of mind.
