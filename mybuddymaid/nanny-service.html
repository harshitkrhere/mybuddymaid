What Does a Professional Nanny Do?

A nanny from MyBuddyMaid provides comprehensive childcare that goes far beyond watching over your child. She is trained to actively contribute to your child's physical, cognitive, and emotional development while ensuring their safety and well-being throughout the day. Here is a detailed breakdown of nanny responsibilities:

Feeding & Nutrition Management

Preparing and serving age-appropriate meals and snacks — bottle feeding for infants, purees and mashed foods for babies, and balanced meals for toddlers and older children. Following the paediatrician's diet chart, tracking feeding times, and ensuring proper hydration throughout the day.

Bathing & Personal Hygiene

Bathing the child safely using appropriate water temperature, baby-safe soaps and shampoos. Maintaining hygiene routines — regular handwashing, nail trimming, hair combing, diaper changes for infants, and toilet training assistance for toddlers.

Sleep Routine & Nap Management

Establishing and maintaining consistent sleep schedules — putting the baby down for naps, soothing fussy infants, reading bedtime stories to toddlers, and ensuring the sleeping environment is safe (no loose blankets, proper crib positioning for infants).

Developmental Play & Activities

Engaging children in age-appropriate activities — tummy time for infants, building blocks and puzzles for toddlers, drawing and colouring for preschoolers, and educational games for older children. Activities are designed to develop motor skills, cognitive abilities, and social awareness.

School & Homework Support

For school-age children: ensuring they are dressed and ready for school on time, packing school bags, helping with homework and revision after school, coordinating with parents about school circulars, and managing extracurricular activity schedules.

Outdoor Supervision & Physical Activity

Taking children for walks in the park or society compound, supervising playground time, organizing outdoor games, and ensuring sun protection and hydration during outdoor activities. Teaching basic road safety when walking outside.

Safety & First Aid Readiness

Childproofing awareness — ensuring sharp objects, medicines, and cleaning chemicals are out of reach. Knowledge of basic first aid — handling minor cuts, falls, and bumps. Awareness of choking hazards for infants and toddlers. Emergency protocol knowledge including nearest hospital details.

Health Monitoring & Medication

Monitoring the child's health — checking for fever, rashes, unusual behaviour, or signs of illness. Administering prescribed medications at correct times and dosages. Maintaining a health log that parents can review daily.

Sibling Management

For families with multiple children: managing the needs of children of different ages simultaneously, mediating sibling conflicts, ensuring equitable attention, and coordinating different meal and nap schedules.

Communication & Reporting to Parents

Providing daily updates to parents — what the child ate, nap times, activities done, any behavioural observations, and milestones achieved. Sharing photos and videos during the day via WhatsApp. Being transparent about any incidents or concerns.

Travel & Outing Support

Accompanying the family on vacations, weekend outings, or family events. Managing the child's needs during travel — packing baby bags, handling feeding and napping on the go, and keeping the child entertained during long journeys.

Types of Nanny & Babysitting Service

Part-Time Nanny (4-6 Hours/Day)

A nanny who comes for a fixed window — typically while parents are at work or during specific hours like morning school routine and after-school supervision. Perfect for families where one parent works from home and needs support during meetings or focused work hours.

Ideal for: Work-from-home parents, families with school-going children, supplemental care when grandparents need a break

Full-Time Nanny (10-12 Hours/Day)

A dedicated nanny who is present from morning until the parents return from work, handling all aspects of childcare throughout the day. She manages the child's complete routine — feeding, bathing, napping, playing, and school support. This is our most comprehensive day-nanny option.

Ideal for: Dual-income families, parents with demanding corporate jobs, families with infants or toddlers under 3

Live-In Nanny (24/7 Residential)

A nanny who resides in your home and is available round the clock, including nighttime duties for infants who need night feeds and diaper changes. She gets a separate room, meals, and weekly off. This is the premium option for families who need complete peace of mind.

Ideal for: Families with newborns, single parents, parents with night shifts or frequent travel, NRI families visiting India with children

Red Flags When Hiring a Nanny

  • 🚩 Candidate shows impatience or irritability when interacting with children during the interview — this only gets worse behind closed doors
  • 🚩 Unwilling to share previous employer references who have children — you must speak to at least one family they worked with
  • 🚩 Excessive reliance on screen time (TV/phone) to manage children — a professional nanny engages actively, not passively
  • 🚩 Cannot describe age-appropriate activities or developmental milestones — indicates lack of childcare knowledge
  • 🚩 Refuses medical fitness certification — non-negotiable for someone who will hold, feed, and bathe your child
  • 🚩 History of job changes every few months — nannies need time to bond with children, and frequent changes are disruptive
  • 🚩 Unable to demonstrate proper infant holding technique or basic feeding practices during the trial
  • 🚩 Shows signs of being on their phone constantly rather than being attentive to the child's needs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a nanny cost in India?
Nanny salaries in India range from ₹12,000 to ₹35,000 per month depending on the city, child's age, number of children, and working hours. Infant care (0-1 year) commands the highest salary — ₹18,000-₹35,000 in metros. Toddler nannies charge ₹15,000-₹28,000. After-school babysitters for older children cost ₹12,000-₹18,000. Live-in nannies are at the premium end of these ranges.
What is the difference between a nanny and a babysitter?
A babysitter provides short-term, temporary care — typically for a few hours while parents go out. A nanny is a long-term, dedicated childcare professional who works set hours daily and is responsible for the child's complete routine including meals, hygiene, education support, and development. MyBuddyMaid provides both — regular nanny placements and on-demand babysitting for occasional needs.
Can I hire a nanny for a newborn?
Yes, and we highly recommend it. Our newborn nannies specialize in infant care — proper latching support for breastfeeding mothers, bottle sterilization, burping techniques, swaddling, recognizing hunger and discomfort cues, and managing sleep cycles. They are experienced in handling the fragile 0-3 month period and gradually transition to more active care as the baby grows.
Is it safe to leave my child alone with a nanny?
With proper verification, yes. Our nannies undergo the most thorough screening in our service lineup — police verification, child-safety background checks, medical fitness certification, and temperament assessment. We also recommend installing CCTV cameras in common areas (living room, children's room) and reviewing footage initially to build confidence. Most parents find that after 2-3 weeks with a good nanny, the anxiety significantly reduces.
What qualifications should a good nanny have?
While formal qualifications like early childhood education degrees are a bonus, the most important qualities are genuine warmth towards children, patience, alertness, hygiene awareness, and experience with children of your child's age. Many of our best nannies are experienced mothers themselves who bring practical, real-world childcare knowledge that no textbook can teach.
Can the nanny help with school homework?
Yes. For school-age children (5+), our nannies assist with homework, ensure school bags are packed, manage school project supplies, and help with basic revision. However, if you need dedicated academic tutoring for specific subjects, that goes beyond a nanny's scope and you may need a separate tutor.
Will the nanny follow my parenting style?
Absolutely. During onboarding, we conduct a detailed discussion about your parenting preferences — discipline approach, screen time rules, food choices, sleep training philosophy, and outdoor play preferences. The nanny is briefed and expected to follow your guidelines consistently. Our relationship manager helps mediate if any alignment issues arise.
I work from home — do I still need a nanny?
More than ever. Working from home with a young child is an impossible juggling act — you cannot type emails while a toddler demands your attention. A nanny allows you to focus on work during business hours while knowing your child is safe, fed, and engaged in the next room. Many work-from-home parents hire part-time nannies for 4-6 hours during their peak work hours.
Can the nanny handle two or more children?
Yes. Many of our nannies have experience managing multiple children of different ages simultaneously. However, if both children are under 2, we strongly recommend a dedicated nanny for each — infants require constant attention and dividing it between two babies compromises care quality. For a toddler plus a school-age child, one experienced nanny can usually manage both.
What if my child does not like the nanny?
This is actually common in the initial days — children, especially toddlers, may resist a new caregiver. We recommend a 5-7 day adjustment period where the parent is present and gradually steps back. However, if after a reasonable adjustment period the child consistently shows distress, clinginess, or behavioural changes, we will immediately replace the nanny. Your child's emotional well-being is the top priority.
