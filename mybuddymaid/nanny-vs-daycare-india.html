For Indian working parents, the childcare question is deeply personal and often stressful. Should you hire a nanny who comes to your home and provides one-on-one care? Or should you enroll your child in a daycare center (crèche) with structured programs and social interaction? There's no universally right answer — it depends on your child's age, your work schedule, your budget, and your family's comfort level.

Choose a Nanny If...

  • Your child is under 1 year (infants need one-on-one care)
  • You or your partner work irregular hours or from home
  • You have more than one child who needs care simultaneously
  • You want your child cared for in the comfort and safety of your home
  • Your child has special needs or medical conditions requiring personalized attention
  • You have elderly family members at home who also need supervision

Choose Daycare If...

  • Your child is 2+ years and needs social interaction with peers
  • You want structured learning activities (pre-school readiness)
  • Your budget is tighter and daycare is more affordable in your area
  • You work standard office hours (9-6) near the daycare facility
  • Your child is outgoing and thrives in group settings

Our Recommendation for Indian Families

Under 2 years: Hire a verified nanny. Infants and toddlers need consistent, one-on-one care from a familiar face. The immunity of very young children is still developing, and daycare exposure to illnesses can be frequent.

2-4 years: A combination works best — nanny in the morning + daycare for 3-4 hours for social/learning activities.

4+ years: Daycare or pre-school during school hours + nanny for after-school care and homework supervision.

FAQs

How much does a nanny cost vs daycare in India?
A full-time nanny in metro cities costs ₹15,000-₹28,000/month, while daycare ranges from ₹8,000-₹25,000/month depending on the facility. However, a nanny provides personalized, flexible care at your home — no commute, no fixed hours, no sending sick children home.
Is a nanny safer than daycare for infants?
For infants under 1 year, a verified nanny at home is generally considered safer. Infants are more vulnerable to infections in group settings, and they need consistent one-on-one bonding. With MyBuddyMaid, every nanny is police-verified and trained in infant care and first aid.
Can I hire a nanny for just after-school hours?
