Real Risks of Hiring Unverified Help

  • Theft: Cash, jewellery, electronics — unverified help with unknown backgrounds have no accountability trail.
  • Child Safety: Unverified nannies around your children is an unacceptable risk. No background check means unknown history.
  • Fraud: Fake identity documents, fabricated work history, and staged references are common in informal hiring.
  • Sudden Disappearance: Unverified help frequently leave without notice, especially after receiving advance salary.
  • No Legal Recourse: Without proper documentation, you have no legal basis to pursue complaints.
